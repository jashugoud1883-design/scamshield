How it works

The analyzer looks for common indicators, including:

⚠️ Urgent language

Threats, deadlines, and pressure to act quickly.

🔐 Sensitive information

Mentions of OTPs, passwords, PINs, or CVVs.

🎁 Prize and payment lures

Unexpected rewards, fees, or requests for money.

🔗 Links and impersonation

URLs and phrases that may imitate trusted services.

The score is a simple demonstration heuristic, not a probability or a verified threat-intelligence result.
